Privacy Policy for Zombie Odyssey

Developer: Wuzzman Entertainment (independent sole proprietorship)
Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Zombie Odyssey handles information when you play the Android game.

Game progress and local storage

Zombie Odyssey is designed to operate offline. Game progress, settings, and save slots are stored on your device. We do not currently intend to require a Wuzzman Entertainment account or synchronize game saves to our servers. Removing the app or clearing its data may delete locally stored progress.

Advertising

The free Android edition is intended to include advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and advertising-related signals to deliver, measure, and protect ads, subject to applicable consent and privacy settings. The precise information processed depends on the SDK configuration, device, region, and choices you make. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Advertising requires an internet connection; the game is designed to remain playable offline.

Purchases

The Android edition may offer an optional purchase to remove ads. Google Play processes purchases and payment information under its own policies. We do not receive your full payment card details. The game may receive purchase status or transaction identifiers needed to grant and restore purchases.

Analytics and diagnostics

We have not yet confirmed whether the release build includes separate analytics or crash-reporting services. This section must be reviewed and updated before publication if such services are included.

Information sharing

We do not sell locally stored game progress. Information processed by advertising and payment providers is governed by their respective services and policies, as described above. We may disclose information when legally required.

Children and age suitability

Zombie Odyssey contains zombie-survival themes. Its intended audience and Google Play age classifications will be set based on the final game's content. If the app is made available to children, we will apply the relevant child privacy and advertising requirements.

Your choices

You can play offline without loading ads, subject to the game's features. Where required, advertising consent and privacy controls will be presented in the app. You can manage certain ad preferences through your device and Google settings. You can remove local game data through the game's controls, if available, or your device's app-storage settings.

Security and changes

We aim to limit data handling to what is necessary for the game and its optional services. No method of electronic processing is completely secure. We may update this policy as the game changes; the date above will reflect revisions.

Zombie Odyssey Privacy Policy

Wuzzman Entertainment

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Wuzzman Entertainment publishes Zombie Odyssey, a zombie-themed survival and strategy game for Android. This policy explains how information may be stored, collected, used, and shared when you play the game.

2. Offline Gameplay and Local Storage

Zombie Odyssey is designed to support offline gameplay. Game progress, character information, inventory, settings, and save slots are stored locally on your device.

The game is designed to operate without requiring a separate Zombie Odyssey account. Local save data is not intentionally uploaded to a Wuzzman Entertainment game server.

3. Advertising

The free Android version of Zombie Odyssey is intended to display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising requires an internet connection.

When AdMob is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and provide advertising services. Personalized advertising may depend on your consent, location, and applicable privacy settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Optional Purchases

Zombie Odyssey may offer an optional purchase to remove advertisements and unlock associated in-game benefits. Purchases are processed through Google Play.

Wuzzman Entertainment does not directly collect or store your payment-card information through the game. Purchase status may be processed to verify and restore purchased benefits.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

The game may use platform services or software components that process technical information necessary for advertising, purchase verification, security, or troubleshooting.

Any additional analytics or crash-reporting services included in the released game will be reflected in this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration.

6. Information Sharing

Wuzzman Entertainment does not sell locally stored game progress or character information.

Information processed by integrated third-party services, including Google AdMob and Google Play, is governed by the applicable service's privacy practices and the choices available to users.

7. Children's Privacy

Zombie Odyssey contains fictional zombie-survival themes. Its intended audience and age suitability will be determined through its Google Play content rating and target-audience declarations.

The game is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Advertising configurations will be selected according to the declared audience and applicable requirements.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can play offline without loading online ads, subject to the game's available features. You can also manage applicable advertising privacy preferences through your device settings and any consent controls presented in the game.

Local game data can generally be removed by deleting saved progress within the game, where supported, or clearing the application's storage through Android settings.

9. Security and Retention

Wuzzman Entertainment aims to limit data handling to what is needed for gameplay and optional services. Locally stored game data remains on the device until removed, subject to device and operating-system behavior.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when game features, advertising services, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this webpage.
